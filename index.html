About

I'm a full-stack developer based in Antwerp, currently finishing the final year of my degree while building real, production websites for real clients. I like projects where the site actually has to work for someone - a customer booking a table, a contractor's client filling out a contact form, a case worker searching a catalog.

My background spans a multilingual company website, a digital library prototype for GO! scholengroep Antwerpen, and my own product built from scratch. I handle a project end to end: design, build, and the unglamorous parts - forms that actually send email, sites that work on a five-year-old phone.
